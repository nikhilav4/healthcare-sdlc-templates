# Project closure report

## When to use it

Write it after hypercare exit and the lessons-learned review, to close the project formally. It records what was delivered against what was approved, hands the remaining work and the tracking of benefits to named owners in the business, and confirms the loose ends are tied off: contracts, access and project data. The sponsor's signature on it releases the team and closes the budget. Keep it factual; the lessons-learned review is the place for discussion.

### 9. Close-out checklist

- [ ] Contracts and purchase orders closed or transferred, and final invoices paid.
- [ ] Project team released, and their managers told.
- [ ] Project and vendor accounts disabled, and admin access granted for the project removed.
- [ ] Test accounts removed from production.
- [ ] Migration extracts, validation spreadsheets and reports containing PHI deleted from shared drives, laptops and email, or moved to an approved location, and the privacy office has confirmed it.
- [ ] Non-production environments cleared of any real patient data, or decommissioned.
- [ ] Project records archived in [location] under the records retention policy.
- [ ] Lessons-learned review held and follow-through owners named.

*Migration projects leave PHI in places nobody tracks: spreadsheets of mismatched records, extracts on a shared drive, screenshots in a chat. Find and remove them before the team disperses and nobody remembers where they are.*
