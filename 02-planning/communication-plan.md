# Communication plan

## When to use it

Write it during planning, working from the stakeholder list in the charter, and review it at every phase gate. It sets out who needs to hear what, from whom, through which channel and how often, and what the project needs back from each group. Most go-live surprises in a hospital come from a group that never got the message: night shift, agency staff, a satellite clinic, the switchboard. Walk the plan through with someone from each clinical area before you trust it.

- Urgent messages during cutover and hypercare go through [the command center] by [overhead page / secure messaging / text alert], not email.
