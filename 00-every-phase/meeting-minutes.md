# Meeting minutes

## When to use it

Use it for any meeting where something gets decided or someone agrees to do something: steering committees, design reviews, vendor checkpoints, defect triage, the go/no-go meeting. Send the minutes within one working day, while people still remember what they agreed to. Minutes matter most months later, when a decision is questioned, so record what was decided and by whom rather than who said what.
