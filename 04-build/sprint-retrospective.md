# Sprint retrospective

## When to use it

Hold it at the end of every sprint, after the sprint review and before planning the next one, for 45 to 90 minutes depending on the sprint's length. The aim is two or three changes the team will actually make next sprint, not a full inventory of what went wrong. Switch formats every few sprints so the conversation doesn't go stale; the two below cover most needs. Open every retro by checking last time's actions, or the team will learn that retro actions don't matter.

## Before the meeting

- [ ] Last retro's actions and where they stand.
- [ ] Sprint facts: goal met or not, committed against completed, carryover, defects found after "done", unplanned work.
- [ ] Input from people who felt the sprint but won't be in the room: clinical reviewers, support, the service desk.

*Bring the numbers to start a conversation, not to grade the team. Two sprints of falling throughput is a question to ask, not a verdict.*

| Start | Stop | Continue |
| --- | --- | --- |

| | | |

### Format B: 4Ls

| Liked | Learned | Lacked | Longed for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [What went well] | [What we know now that we didn't before] | [What was missing: information, access, time, people] | [What we wish we'd had] |
| | | | |

*Group similar notes, then vote on two or three to act on. Everything else is recorded and left alone this time.*

### Actions

| # | Action | Owner | Due | How we'll know it worked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [S12-1] | [A specific change; starts with a verb] | [One named person] | [Date or next sprint] | [Something observable] |

*If nobody will own an action by the end of the meeting, drop it. Listing it anyway teaches the team that the list means nothing.*

## Action tracker

Keep one running table across sprints so patterns show. The same problem raised three sprints in a row needs a bigger fix or an escalation, not a fourth action.

| # | Raised in sprint | Action | Owner | Due | Status | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [S12-1] | [12] | | | | [Open / Done / Dropped] | [What changed] |

*Some themes aren't the team's to fix: a test environment refresh that wipes UAT data, slow security reviews, clinical reviewers who can't get released from the floor. Raise those in the [RAID log](../02-planning/raid-log.md) with an owner outside the team.*
